The problem

Having a health concern shouldn't mean navigating the internet alone. Sensitive questions are hard to say out loud — so most people just don't.

Difficult to discuss

Unreliable information

Unclear next steps

Many don't know whether a concern needs professional attention at all.

Late-stage care

Healthcare often starts only after someone has already decided to seek it.
